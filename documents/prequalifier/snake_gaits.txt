Title: Motion Planning and Simulation of Snake Robots

Brief intro of the project:
Snake robots are a class of hyper-redundant degree-of-freedom (DoF) robots. In the study of motion control, it is challenging to intuitively imagine the robot's overall pose solely from joint angle data. Furthermore, conducting direct hardware experiments can be risk-prone and cost-prohibitive, potentially leading to hardware damage and significant time loss. Therefore, extensive simulations are essential in the development process. This project focuses on reproducing locomotion gaits within a simulated environment.

Job description/workload
The student's work involves implementing a motion planning algorithm for snake-like robots according to a research paper and conducting simulations on the MuJoCo platform.
The workload is expected to be at least 12 hours per week. 

Keywords of this project
Robotics, Motion Planning, Simulation
