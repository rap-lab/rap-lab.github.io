Dun Yan Ong is pursuing a bachelor's degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at [Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/) (NTU), Singapore. He was exchanged to SJTU for a semester in 2025, and become an undergraduate researcher in the lab since then.
His research interests are in multi-robot systems operating in unknown environments, including ground robots and drones.
