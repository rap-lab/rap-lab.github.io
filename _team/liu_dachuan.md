Dachuan Liu is an undergraduate research assistant at the [Global College](https://www.gc.sjtu.edu.cn/). He is a dual-degree student in Mechanical Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan. His research interests are multi-agent path finding with asynchronous actions and planning for large agents.
