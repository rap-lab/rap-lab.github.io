Yuebo Hu is an undergraduate researcher at the Global College. He is pursuing a dual degree in CS at the UMich and ECE at SJTU. His research focuses on Mixed-Integer Programming (MIP) in motion planning for multi-robot systems and tethered robots. Potential applications include warehouse automation and low-altitude aerial operations.
